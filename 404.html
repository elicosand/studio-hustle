Bring your career with you

  1. Open the new address and add it to your Home Screen (iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen).
  2. Open the new icon. Tap ⚙️ Settings, scroll to Cloud save and tap Already protected? Load my career.
  3. Enter the email you protected your progress with, then type the 6-digit code we email you.
  4. On Cloud save found, tap Load cloud save. (Not "Keep this device's save", which would replace your career with the new blank one.) Your albums, outfits, gems and Charts listing come back.
  5. Delete this old icon from your Home Screen.
